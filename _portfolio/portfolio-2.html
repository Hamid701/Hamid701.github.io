What the project explores

For years, predicting football matches has mixed team form, personal judgment, and luck. BetWinner takes a structured approach by bringing several information sources into one prediction workflow.

Match data

Historical results and team statistics provide the quantitative foundation for each prediction.

News sentiment

News signals add context about how teams and players are being discussed before a match.

Probability model

A logistic-regression model estimates probabilities for win, draw, and loss outcomes.

Telegram interface

A conversational bot guides users through team selection and presents predictions and visualizations.

Responsible use: BetWinner is a data-science experiment, not a gambling or financial-decision tool. Its predictions depend on the quality and availability of the underlying data.
